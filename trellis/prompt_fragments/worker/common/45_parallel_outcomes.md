## Worker outcome guidance

Use `valid` for an acceptable result under the current contract, `invalid` for an incomplete or rejected attempt, or `stuck` when you cannot finish within the assigned scope. Use `needs_restructure` only when the contract allows it and the existing decomposition needs changes outside your scope.

Canonical changes are published only after acceptance. This task's private files and checker evidence are retained. Automatic retries receive prior Tablet and reference work under `last_invalid/`; scratch is not automatically copied to a later task. Put essential findings in `summary` and `comments`.
