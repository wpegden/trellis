## Conditional theorem obligations (trust protocol v1)

An entry at `request_summary.trust_conditional_theorem_candidates` exists only after the kernel has activated a claim-B conditional for a Decide target (Lane 3, via the authorized deviation lane). The map key and each `node_id` identify the active Tablet proof node; `statement_utf8` is the exact kernel-sealed Lean statement. Prove a visible, scheduled candidate through the ordinary ProofFormalization worker/checker/local-closure path.

A conditional candidate does not replace, discharge, weaken, or rewrite its `target_id` unrestricted target. Keep both obligations and their challenge claims intact. Do not turn the unrestricted target into the conditional statement, and do not prove the candidate by importing or invoking the unrestricted theorem.

`Tablet/Preamble.{lean,tex}` and `Tablet/Assumptions.{lean,tex}` are immutable seed-owned support carriers in a required-v1 run. They are readable dependencies, not worker-authored theorem nodes, and their appearance in a target's dependency/impact closure does not grant edit authority. Never modify, delete, replace, rename, or report them as new nodes. Put new proof support in an ordinary registered node. A change to any of these files rides the audited seam-repair deviation lane and is rejected before semantic validation otherwise.

For source-counterexample work, use only `request_summary.trust_source_validation_guidance[target_id]`. Its `validation_method`, `public_entry_point`, and exact `concretization_schema_utf8` are seed-authenticated execution requirements. A source witness or descriptor must satisfy that exact schema; configuration prose or an ad-hoc substitute is not authority.

This rule applies to every configured Decide claim, including supporting claims; it is not special to the campaign's headline target. A checked Lean refutation establishes only that the extracted-model claim is false. Never describe a model-admissible witness as a real Rust execution, allocation, or source counterexample unless the target's frozen validation method produces the corresponding authenticated source result. If the exact runner cannot start or complete the source call, preserve that reason; the kernel routes the pair through the recorded station verdict (model repair, claim-B conditional, or loud give-up). Do not infer source realizability merely from `SliceBytes`, a carrier predicate, or a large symbolic descriptor.
