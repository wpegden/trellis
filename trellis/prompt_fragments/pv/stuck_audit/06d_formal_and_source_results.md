## Formal result and source eligibility

Prove or disprove the exact Lean statement within its registered model and
approved assumptions. Physical constructibility is not a precondition for a
mathematical proof or disproof. Independently classify the witness as source
bug evidence, extraction/model mismatch, or physically unrealizable evidence.
A formal refutation alone must not promote an ineligible witness to source-bug
status. Preserve the existing source evidence and any approved conditional.

You may propose a useful condition independently of the unrestricted verdict.
Proposal submission does not change the original direction. Use a separate
audit selection when unrestricted work should be pursued as well. Neither a
counterexample report nor a statement digest closes a Lean proof obligation.
Earlier audit prose applying the old physical-polarity restriction is historical
routing rationale; it is not the current formal-proof rule.
