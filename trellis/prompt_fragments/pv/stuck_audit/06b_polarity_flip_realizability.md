## What a disprove requires

A `disprove` rests on a witness the eligibility rubric classes as constructible
on a well-provisioned present-day machine. When the only refuting inputs need
exotic or possibly nonexisting hardware, leave `set_live_polarity` empty and
file the `conditional_theorem_proposal` the proposal section describes, with a
condition that excludes the witness class and the hardware property that class
demands in `rationale`; both unconditional sides stay open and the human
ratifies the condition at the theorem-stating advance gate.
