## Modeling and goal stating (Phase 0)

This phase states the GOALs faithfully over the extracted model, making only necessary adaptations and additions. Setup and adaptation end with audits. Modeling choices require preservation proofs with goal-specific applications. Completion requires an elaborated model and goal coverage, current semantic reviews, and final audit. Outside-expert approval of the model, scope, and goal meanings starts argument development. GOAL proof, disproof, and truth classification belong to argument development.
