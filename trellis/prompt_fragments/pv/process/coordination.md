## Coordination and exceptions

The reviewer coordinates work and requests transitions. Workers edit artifacts, verifiers apply their lane criteria to the current artifacts, and audits decide designated completion and exception requests. The system enforces acceptance gates against the current artifacts.

Consequential scope reductions, GOAL declines, and substantive model computation receive two modeling-route planner/critic rounds and a final audit before commitment, in preparation or native modeling. Goals remain pursued until the audit approves a route, a justified decline, or failure. Route approvals authorize scope and implementation choices, with preservation obligations. Within the approved scope, retained GOALs determine the target statements; verifiers judge their faithfulness independently of prior agent judgments. During adaptation, the entire candidate crate remains editable; changing the accepted extraction configuration or environment requires setup reopening. Adaptation acceptance freezes the source and extraction basis; later changes require audit-approved reopening and revalidation of affected work. Authored model definitions use the ordinary node revision and semantic review machinery. If a necessary change undermines the basis of downstream work beyond coherent recovery, the run ends with a process failure.
