When weighing narrower scope or declined GOALs against the additional hand-authoring needed to retain them, assess whether the resulting model’s informally justified correspondence to the source would make successful formalization convincing evidence for its conclusions about the program. Prefer wider intended scope and retaining GOALs where this standard can be met; otherwise seek the smallest reduction that permits it.

Substantive model computation requires route review when it makes consequential choices about how source operations are represented, the retained behavior, or the preservation argument needed to connect them. Ordinary vocabulary used to state GOALs within an approved model does not by itself require a new route review. A definition's purpose and semantic effect determine this distinction, not its size or whether it is newly authored.
