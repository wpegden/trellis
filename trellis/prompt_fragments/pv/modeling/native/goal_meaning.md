Modeling establishes a source-faithful model and states each pursued `GOAL.md`
obligation with its original meaning. Read that obligation directly. A
"positive statement" means the original obligation, including any negation
already present in it; it does not mean the direction expected to be true.

Counterexamples to a supplied GOAL are evidence for later argument development.
They do not authorize replacing its principal statement by a negation, weakening
it, or declining it solely because it appears false. Keep that evidence as
diagnostic context. Model preservation and claims used to justify the model must
still be established: a false preservation or support claim is a repair blocker.

Route approvals govern modeling scope, implementation, and preservation work.
They do not select proof/disproof direction or override GOAL meaning. Check route
tasks against the original obligations; return a conflicting task for correction
through the existing modeling review. In particular, a route approval written
in prose does not perform a kernel polarity change. The system creates authored
goal pairs after Modeling approval; its existing argument-development mechanism
then selects and verifies a direction without rewriting the original obligation.

The completion audit must compare every principal statement against its original
GOAL, independently of earlier approvals. Reject reversed or otherwise changed
obligations with `modeling_revise`. This is a meaning check, not a test of whether
the original GOAL is true.
