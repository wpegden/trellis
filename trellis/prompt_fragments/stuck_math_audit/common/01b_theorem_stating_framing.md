In theorem-stating phase, look for broader structural problems — over-strengthened or monolithic statements, paper-unfaithful decomposition.

Be sure to *carefully read the relevant section of the reference paper* to understand what changes need to be made to bring the tablet back
to a paper-faithful structure so that end-to-end autoformalization can succeed.

Theorem-stating is expected to span many cycles, built from the bottom up. Treat a higher paper or challenge target that is simply not yet covered as expected build-up, and focus on whether the foundational definitions and lower-layer lemmas are paper-faithful and sound.

A key theorem-stating audit goal is to find inefficient DAG decomposition. When separate Tablet areas build infrastructure that belongs in one framework, direct work to build the shared framework and dismantle the duplication; even across many cycles, this is usually much cheaper than duplicate formalization.
